On June 15, 2026, our lab meeting was held at the MUST Innovation Technology Research Institute in the Hengqin-Macao In-Depth Cooperation Zone. During the meeting, we discussed recent progress in our ongoing projects, reviewed experimental plans, and aligned the next steps for our research on intestinal disease, organoid models, and regenerative medicine.

It was a productive opportunity for the team to exchange ideas, strengthen collaboration, and continue building our research platform in Hengqin. The Innovation Technology Research Institute provides an excellent environment for cutting-edge research and fosters interdisciplinary collaboration between Macau and the mainland.

The meeting covered a wide range of topics, including updates on biliary atresia studies, intestinal organoid development, liver fibrosis models, and tissue injury repair mechanisms. Team members presented their latest data and received constructive feedback from colleagues, contributing to the refinement of ongoing experimental strategies.

As our research presence in Hengqin continues to grow, this gathering marks another important step in building a robust platform for translational medicine research in the Greater Bay Area.
