The Bo Li Laboratory at the Precision Regenerative Medicine Research Centre (PRMRC), Macau University of Science and Technology (MUST), is pleased to announce the international symposium **"Frontiers in Science: A Youth-Led Vanguard International Symposium on Healthy Aging,"** to be held in Macao from November 1 to 4, 2026.

The symposium is a youth-led initiative that brings together early-career researchers and leading scientists to explore the frontiers of healthy aging research. The program spans fundamental mechanisms of aging, age-related diseases, and emerging therapeutic and regenerative strategies, with a focus on interdisciplinary approaches and the translation of research into improved health across the life course.

![Frontiers in Science: A Youth-Led Vanguard International Symposium on Healthy Aging](https://github.com/pilaobanmust-sketch/lab-website/blob/main/images/healthy-aging-symposium-banner.png?raw=true)

The four-day program features keynote lectures, invited talks, and presentations from young investigators addressing topics at the intersection of aging, stem cell biology, regenerative medicine, and related disciplines. By placing young scientists at the forefront of the scientific program, the symposium aims to foster the next generation of leaders in healthy aging research and to strengthen international collaboration among emerging and established researchers.

Further details on the program and participation will be shared as the event approaches. We warmly welcome colleagues interested in healthy aging and regenerative medicine to join us in Macao this November.
