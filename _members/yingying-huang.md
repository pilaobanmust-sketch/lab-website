Huang Yingying is a PhD Student at the Bo Li Laboratory. She earned her Master of Medicine degree from Fudan University and was honored as an Outstanding Graduate of Fudan University in 2026.

## Research Interests

Her research focuses on necrotizing enterocolitis (NEC), with particular emphasis on:

- **Immunonutrient-mediated intestinal repair in NEC** — Investigating the mechanisms by which immunonutrients promote intestinal epithelial repair in NEC, as well as their potential for clinical translation.
- **Intestinal organoid- and extracellular vesicle-based therapy for NEC** — Exploring the therapeutic effects and underlying mechanisms of intestinal organoid transplantation and organoid-derived extracellular vesicles in NEC.

## Personal Interests

Outside the lab, Yingying enjoys traveling, hiking, reading, and taking care of small animals.
