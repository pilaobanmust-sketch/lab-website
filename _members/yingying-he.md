Yingying (Jasmine) He is a PhD Candidate at the Faculty of Medicine, University of Macau. She earned her MMD from China Medical University in Liaoning, Shenyang.

## Research Interests

Her research focuses on biliary atresia, with particular emphasis on:

1. **Human Amniotic Fluid Stem Cells (hAFSCs)-Mediated Mitochondrial Transfer**: Investigating the therapeutic potential and mechanisms in biliary atresia via bile duct ligation (BDL) model.

2. **Dynamic Profiling of Bile Acid Metabolism**: Studying bile acid metabolism in the intestine of children with biliary atresia, both before and after surgery.

3. **Postoperative Cholangitis**: Understanding the causes and pathogenetic mechanisms of frequent cholangitis in children with biliary atresia after surgery.
