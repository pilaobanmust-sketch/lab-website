MUST Signs Memorandum of Understanding with Nazarbayev University to Strengthen International Medical Collaboration
Bo Li Principal Investigator Macau University of Science and Technology
October 05, 2026 October 20, 2026
collaboration international-cooperation memorandum-of-understanding medical-education regenerative-medicine

On October 5, 2026, Professor Paul Kwong-Hang Tam, Vice President of Macau University of Science and Technology (MUST), and Dr. Bo Li, Assistant Professor at the Precision Regenerative Medicine Research Centre (PRMRC), represented MUST at the international medical seminar “Global Medical Frontiers: Innovation, Resilience and Sharing” held in Astana, Kazakhstan. The seminar was jointly organized by the GX Foundation, the Ministry of Health of the Republic of Kazakhstan, and Nazarbayev University (NU). During the event, MUST and NU formally signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU), opening new opportunities for collaboration in medical education, scientific research, and talent development.

MUST Researchers Visit SickKids for Academic Exchange and Present at the 57th CAPS Annual Meeting
Bo Li Principal Investigator Macau University of Science and Technology
September 24, 2026 October 20, 2026
conference caps sickkids pediatric-surgery biliary-atresia academic-exchange

In September 2026, Dr. Bo Li, Assistant Professor (Research) at the Precision Regenerative Medicine Research Centre (PRMRC) of Macau University of Science and Technology (MUST), and his PhD student, Yingying He, travelled to Canada for an academic exchange at The Hospital for Sick Children (SickKids), affiliated with the University of Toronto, and participated in international paediatric surgery academic activities. During the visit, He’s research was selected as one of the 10 best abstracts for the Surgeons Den session at the 57th Annual Meeting of the Canadian Association of Paediatric Surgeons (CAPS), where she delivered an oral presentation, highlighting MUST’s research in paediatric diseases and regenerative medicine.
